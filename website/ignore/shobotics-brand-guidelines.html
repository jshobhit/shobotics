03 01 · Brand Essence

Who we are.

Shobotics is a systems-first technology company operating at the intersection of neurotechnology, artificial intelligence, consciousness research, and creative media.

Brand Idea

Precise systems, imperfect human signal.

The visual language combines technical precision with human imperfection. It should feel intelligent, minimal, experimental, and alive — like a precise lab notebook from the future, touched by a human hand.

Design Philosophy

Shobotics maintains a visual tension between three forces: the hand-crafted and human, the scientific and technical, and the mystic and experimental. No single force dominates. The system exists in the balance.

Personality Keywords

Precise Conscious Experimental Human Technical Minimal Premium Slightly punk Calm Future-facing Disciplined

The brand is

  • Minimal — but not empty
  • Technical — but not sterile
  • Human — but not messy
  • Experimental — but not vague
  • Premium — but not corporate
  • Slightly punk — but disciplined

The brand is not

  • Generic SaaS
  • Neon cyberpunk
  • Wellness spa cliché
  • Corporate consulting deck
  • Gamer-tech aesthetic
  • Overdesigned spiritual branding

05 03 · Logo System

The wordmark.

The Shobotics wordmark is a handwritten logotype. It must always be used as supplied artwork — never rebuilt, retyped, or approximated with any font. The irregular stroke contrast and organic letterforms are properties of the original, not properties of any typeface.

Licensing Note

The Shobotics wordmark must always be used as artwork. Exact Adobe font names may be substituted only with approved licensed equivalents — but never for the logo itself. The wordmark must never be rebuilt manually.

14 10 · Layout & Grid

Space is signal.

Whitespace is a core brand asset — not leftover space. The layout should feel sparse, deliberate, and intelligent. Density is earned through meaning, not decoration.

Grid Specification

WEB
12-column grid · generous margins
DECK
16:9 or 4:3 · white canvas default
PRINT
A4 / Letter · 20mm margins minimum
SOCIAL
1:1 or 9:16 · minimal content per frame

Composition Rules

  • →One dominant visual idea per page
  • →Use asymmetry with balance — not chaos
  • →Thin rules and light dividers only
  • →Contour motif in corners or as large cropped detail
  • →Lavender footer band for documents and decks

15 11 · Signal Field System

Neural Topography.

The proprietary graphic language of Shobotics.

Concept

The contour-line system represents signal, attention, neural terrain, invisible systems, and organic computation. It is the visual DNA connecting all Shobotics outputs — from document footers to hero sections.

Lines should feel organic and topographic — like cartographic contours mapping cognitive terrain — not like stock circuit boards or generic tech wireframes.

Visual Rules

  • →Use thin contour-like strokes only
  • →Lines may be black, grey, white, or soft lavender
  • →Low opacity when placed behind text
  • →Dense black linework only as hero visual — never behind body copy
  • →Lines encode complexity — not decoration

Usage Modes

Heading Subtle bg — dark
Heading Subtle bg — light corner crop
Hero field Hero — full opacity, cropped
Accent Purple accent field

Avoid

✕ Generic AI brain icons
✕ Circuit board patterns
✕ Glowing blue neural networks
✕ Dense patterns behind text

18 14 · Motion Language

System coming
into coherence.

Motion is a signal, not a spectacle.

Reveal

Elements enter from below or fade from opacity 0 → 1. Duration: 600–900ms. Easing: cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)

Slow · Deliberate
Line draw

Signal field contours animate via SVG stroke-dasharray/dashoffset. Draw over 1.2–2s. Simulate sensing/scanning behavior.

Signature motion
Pulse

Subtle opacity oscillation for active signal states. Amplitude: ±0.08 opacity. Cycle: 3–5s. Never jarring.

Ambient
Transition

Page / view transitions: cross-fade preferred. No slide-in theatrics. Match the brand pace — calm, not anxious.

Smooth

Avoid

✕ Aggressive glitch effects

✕ Fast cyberpunk cuts

✕ Generic HUD animations

✕ Random bouncing UI elements

✕ Excessive parallax

Easing Reference

reveal: cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)

exit: cubic-bezier(0.55, 0.06, 0.68, 0.19)

transition: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)

Duration range: 300ms – 1200ms

21 Quick Reference

Do / Don't.

Do
  • ✓Use generous whitespace as a design element
  • ✓Use the logo as supplied artwork only
  • ✓Use purple sparingly — as an accent, not a flood
  • ✓Use thin linework aligned with Signal Field weight
  • ✓Use black for clarity and structure
  • ✓Use lavender for support surfaces and footer bands
  • ✓Keep layouts calm, precise, and intentional
  • ✓Let one dominant idea control each page
  • ✓Use Cormorant Garamond for display headings
  • ✓Use IBM Plex Mono for all technical labels
Don't
  • ✕Rebuild the logo from a font
  • ✕Overuse handwritten typography
  • ✕Use neon gradients or rainbow palettes
  • ✕Use generic AI blue / glowing circuit imagery
  • ✕Crowd the page with multiple competing elements
  • ✕Add heavy drop shadows
  • ✕Use purple for body text
  • ✕Look like corporate SaaS, wellness spa, or gamer-tech
  • ✕Flood entire layouts with saturated colour
  • ✕Use fast, chaotic animations
