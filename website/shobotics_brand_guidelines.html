01 Brand Essence

Precise systems, imperfect human signal.

Shobotics is rigorous technology touched by a human hand.

Shobotics operates at the intersection of systems engineering, neurotechnology, artificial intelligence, visual culture, and human consciousness. The brand should feel intelligent, minimal, experimental, calm, premium, and slightly punk without becoming chaotic.

Brand personality

Precise Conscious Experimental Human Technical Minimal Premium Slightly punk Calm Future-facing

The brand is

  • Minimal but not empty.
  • Technical but not sterile.
  • Human but not messy.
  • Experimental but not vague.
  • Premium but not corporate.
  • Slightly punk but disciplined.

The brand is not

  • Generic SaaS or startup blue.
  • Neon cyberpunk or gamer-tech.
  • Wellness cliché or spiritual stock art.
  • Corporate consulting deck language.
  • Overdesigned mystical branding.
  • AI brain icons and circuit-board clichés.

Rules

  • Use the official handwritten wordmark supplied as SVG, PDF, or transparent PNG.
  • Do not rebuild the logo using a font, even if the font appears similar.
  • Minimum clear space equals the height of the lowercase “s” around all sides.
  • Digital minimum width: 120 px. Print minimum width: 25 mm.

Background control

  • Use purple or black logo on white/light backgrounds.
  • Use white logo on black/dark backgrounds.
  • Place inside a clean container when background linework is dense.
  • Never sacrifice legibility for aesthetic texture.

03 Colour System

White space, black signal, purple charge.

Shobotics Purple

#AE72F9
Brand accent, active states, small highlights, CTAs.

Signal Black

#000000
Primary text, diagrams, linework, high-contrast structure.

Soft Lavender

#EAC7F0
Footer bands, subtle panels, dividers, quiet emphasis.

System White

#FFFFFF
Dominant background and negative space.

Recommended proportion across most applications: 70% white / negative space, 20% black typography and linework, 8% lavender support, 2% saturated purple accent.

Use

  • Purple sparingly for hierarchy and memory.
  • Black for diagrams, serious text, and precision.
  • Lavender for soft surfaces, footer bands, and section rhythm.
  • White as the default state of the brand.

Avoid

  • Full-page purple unless explicitly intentional.
  • Startup-blue, neon green, rainbow gradients, and random accents.
  • Low-contrast lavender text on white.
  • Generic sci-fi glow treatments.

04 Typography

Define type by role, not dependency.

Because the original typography may be Adobe-native, do not make the design system dependent on unavailable font files. Preserve the wordmark as artwork and use licensed, replaceable fonts for all editable text.

LOGO / LOCKED ASSET

Use only supplied logo artwork. Do not rebuild manually.

DISPLAY / HEADINGS
Elegant, quiet, editorial intelligence.

Fallback: Cormorant Garamond Light, Playfair Display, Fraunces, or equivalent licensed display face.

BODY / READING
Use a clean modern sans for readable documents, websites, technical notes, and investor material.

Fallback: Inter, Helvetica, Neue Haas Grotesk, Avenir, or equivalent.

TECHNICAL / DATA
COGSTATE_PROB 0.72 · SIGNAL_QUALITY GOOD · LOOP_LATENCY 41ms

Fallback: IBM Plex Mono, Space Mono, JetBrains Mono, or equivalent.

05 Layout & Grid

Controlled asymmetry with breathing room.

Layouts should feel like a precise lab notebook from the future: spacious, structured, slightly asymmetrical, and never overfilled. The contour system may occupy large areas, but information must remain calm and legible.

  • Use a 12-column grid for web and presentation pages.
  • Use large margins and avoid edge-to-edge clutter.
  • One dominant idea per page or screen.
  • Prefer asymmetry, but anchor it with strong alignment.
  • Standardize footer bands for confidential documents.
Example slide

One idea, plenty of air.

The graphic system supports the message. It should not compete with it.

Margins

Keep generous outer margins. Do not let text touch edges or line-art clusters.

Alignment

Use consistent left alignment for content-heavy pages. Center only for covers or intentional pauses.

Density

Documents may be comprehensive, but pages should never feel like dashboards unless they are UI screens.

06 Graphic Language

Neural topography / signal field.

The contour-line motif is the most ownable graphic device in the current brand language. Treat it as a formal system, not a random decoration.

Rules

  • Use thin black or light grey contour lines.
  • Forms should feel organic, neural, topographic, or field-like.
  • Use low opacity when placed behind text.
  • Crop asymmetrically for premium compositions.
  • Use purple only as rare signal emphasis, not as linework default.

Approved

Thin contour fields, neural flows, terrain-like signal maps, quiet generative patterns.

Avoid

Stock AI brains, circuit boards, chrome robots, neon waves, generic crypto gradients.

Opacity

5–15% behind text. 40–100% only as hero artwork or cropped edge motif.

07 Imagery, Illustration & Iconography

Human, technical, never stock-generic.

Photography

  • Minimal, high-contrast, quiet, premium.
  • Human but not corporate.
  • Prefer hands, devices, materials, labs, creative process, rituals.
  • Avoid over-smiling wellness clichés and startup stock photos.

Illustration

  • Thin line-based and organic.
  • Sketchbook meets systems diagram.
  • Minimal colour, mostly black/white/purple.
  • Slightly imperfect, but not messy.

Iconography

  • 24 px base grid.
  • Thin consistent stroke.
  • Rounded but precise.
  • No filled cartoon icons.
  • No overly literal AI/neuro clichés.

Product / 3D direction

Use matte surfaces, soft shadows, minimal environments, and black/white/purple control. Do not create glossy sci-fi renders unless the context deliberately calls for speculative art.

Composition direction

Imagery should feel observational and authored. It should show systems, process, attention, and tactile intelligence rather than generic “innovation” symbolism.

09 Motion Language

Systems coming into coherence.

Motion should be slow, deliberate, and signal-like. It should never feel like generic cyberpunk energy or aggressive glitch aesthetics.

  • Use gradual reveals, fades, line drawing, and subtle drift.
  • Animate contour fields like slow signal stabilization.
  • Keep UI interactions responsive but calm.
  • Use micro-motion to show focus, not spectacle.
  • Avoid fast glitch, noisy flicker, excessive parallax, and gaming HUD effects.

Motion metaphor

A system finding signal through noise.

Best used for website hero sections, product UI state changes, deck transitions, and AI film visual identity.

10 Voice & Tone

Intelligent without startup fluff.

Shobotics sounds like

  • Intelligent but not academic.
  • Experimental but not vague.
  • Calm but not passive.
  • Premium but not pretentious.
  • Human but not sloppy.
  • Slightly punk, but disciplined.

Preferred phrases

  • Conscious technology.
  • Systems for attention, perception, and creative intelligence.
  • Built with precision, designed for humans.
  • Signal over noise.
  • Human-first systems engineering.

Avoid

  • “Revolutionary AI-powered solution.”
  • “Disrupting everything.”
  • “Unlock your full potential.”
  • “Next-gen seamless ecosystem.”
  • Buzzwords without proof.

Tone rule

Speak as if explaining a sophisticated system to an intelligent person. Be direct, precise, and visually restrained. The brand can be strange, but the language must stay clear.

11 Brand Architecture

One parent system, multiple expressions.

Shobotics

Parent technology company. Uses the full visual system: wordmark, contour field, purple/black/white palette, technical-human tone.

Dhyaan

Neurotech / mindfulness wearable. May inherit the system with calmer surfaces, more physiological/data language, and softer human interface cues.

Shobotics Studio

AI film and creative technology arm. May use more expressive motion, cinematic cropping, and bolder applications while retaining the core system.

14 Do / Don’t Summary

Final consistency checks.

Do

  • Use the official wordmark artwork.
  • Keep white space dominant.
  • Use black linework and technical clarity.
  • Use purple as a memorable accent.
  • Use contour fields as the signature visual system.
  • Write with precision and restraint.
  • Show systems, process, signal, attention, and human intelligence.

Don’t

  • Turn the brand into generic AI/SaaS visuals.
  • Overuse purple or neon gradients.
  • Use stock AI brains, chrome robots, or circuit-board clichés.
  • Let handwritten typography dominate readable content.
  • Overcrowd pages with dashboards or decorative noise.
  • Use vague startup claims without substance.
  • Recreate proprietary Adobe fonts without a license.

The brand should feel like a precise lab notebook from the future, touched by a human hand.
