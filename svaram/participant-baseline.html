About this form and your download

This form records how you feel before the session. It does not calculate an overall health score.

The downloaded file keeps each answer separate. Skipped questions stay unanswered. For the 0–10 questions, a higher number means more of that feeling, including stress and sleepiness.

The download is a JSON data file for the study team. It includes your codes, the questions, your answers and the times they were recorded. The team can use your session code to match your answers with your recording.
