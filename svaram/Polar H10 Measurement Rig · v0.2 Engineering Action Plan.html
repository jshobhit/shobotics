01

Scope & boundaries

Build

The application shall record one participant through one Polar H10 on a MacBook. It shall store local raw streams, events, optional audio, and an optional baseline survey.

Window 1 shall control acquisition. Window 2 shall inspect stored sessions. Each finalized, successfully analysed session shall contain a portable report.html.

Preserve

Raw measurements shall remain primary data. Analysis shall remain reproducible derived data.

The agent shall reuse the existing recorder, BLE integration, and supported export paths where they satisfy this contract. Existing session folders shall not be renamed or rewritten automatically.

Explicitly out of scope

Cloud sync; accounts; remote dashboards; remote databases; patient-record systems; hospital/PACS integration; multi-participant or multi-H10 recording; complex study management; diagnostic ECG interpretation; causal or significance claims; custom analysis ranges; historical label editing; raw-data editing; overlapping labels; frequency-domain HRV research tools; embedded questionnaire authoring; new survey scoring; live heavy HRV or respiration analysis.

No framework migration, database replacement, or unrelated redesign is authorized by this revamp. Optional post-session backup is not a new v0.2 deliverable.

Measured & derived metrics

HRV shall be a primary displayed metric. Its provenance shall remain derived from beat intervals. Respiration shall be identified as ECG-derived respiration estimate.

  • MET-01The engine shall retain original RR values. It shall record exclusions or corrections separately. It shall not silently substitute ECG-derived intervals for device RR.
  • MET-02The engine shall calculate full-session summaries and eligible contiguous label-segment summaries. It shall identify repeated occurrences of the same label separately.
  • MET-03The engine shall not form successive RR pairs across pauses, missing data, rejected intervals, or disconnected segments. Label-specific calculations shall also stop at label boundaries.
  • MET-04The engine shall return null with a reason when duration, beat count, or quality is insufficient. The UI shall show “Insufficient data” or the specific reason.
  • MET-05The engine shall store analysis windows, cleaning rules, minimum durations, quality thresholds, and algorithm versions. The same inputs and configuration shall reproduce the same numeric results within a declared tolerance.
  • MET-06The engine shall calculate plot reductions after recording. Reduced plot data shall not replace source samples or determine summary statistics.
Definition gate. Freeze SDNN variance convention, pNN50 denominator, mean-HR method, coverage denominator, RR cleaning, and eligible window lengths before implementation. Do not invent clinical limits. Compare repeated labels descriptively; do not concatenate separated intervals into a false continuous record.

07

Label model

  • LAB-01The session shall have one primary active label while recording. Start shall open a Silence segment at time zero.
  • LAB-02A label change shall close the current segment and open the next at the same session timestamp. Segments shall not overlap.
  • LAB-03Each segment shall retain a stable ID, label ID, start time, and end time. The manifest shall retain the label names and report styling map.
  • LAB-04Label controls shall be disabled in PAUSED and all non-recording states. Stop shall close the open segment. Historical label editing shall be unavailable.
  • LAB-05Reports shall display pauses and data gaps separately from labels. Label coverage shall not imply valid physiological coverage.

Protocol examples: Silence, Gong, Voice, Instrument A, Instrument B, Transition. These names are examples except for the required default Silence.

Proposed boundary behavior: ignore repeated clicks on the active label; close its segment at Pause; open a new segment with the retained label at Resume. Keep the label through a BLE dropout and overlay the data gap. Confirm these defaults in Phase 0.

08

report.html behavior

  • REP-01Stop shall trigger finalization, analysis, and report generation in that order. The renderer shall publish report.html only after a successful write.
  • REP-02The report shall contain embedded styles, required scripts, and compact derived plot data. It shall open locally without a network connection or running Dashboard.
  • REP-03The report shall not embed the full raw recording. It shall identify the session, analysis version, source, and recording completeness.
  • REP-04The report shall show Highlights at the top: duration, valid ECG coverage, mean HR, RMSSD, SDNN, estimated respiration rate, label count, and recording quality. Unavailable metrics shall show a reason.
  • REP-05The document structure and Highlights shall appear immediately. Charts shall initialize near the viewport. A hidden tab shall initialize its charts when opened. Scrolling shall not trigger analysis.
  • REP-06The report shall retain gaps in plots. Every time-series chart shall show labels behind its trace. Text and a legend shall explain labels and gaps.
  • REP-07The report shall be descriptive. It shall not diagnose a condition or claim that a stimulus caused a physiological change.
  • REP-08The application shall regenerate missing or failed reports from stored valid data. Regeneration shall retain raw checksums. Recovered-session reports shall state missing coverage.
  • REP-09The renderer shall escape imported text and embedded data. Patient, facilitator, label, and survey values shall not execute as HTML or script.

Implementation direction: viewport intersection detection for chart initialization; precomputed plot reductions; embedded fallback summaries; atomic report replacement. Printing shall make all report sections available. A rendering error shall expose an error message without altering the session.

Session state machine

IDLE → READY → RECORDING ⇄ PAUSED
                    └────────┴→ FINALIZING → COMPLETE
Active-session interruption → INCOMPLETE → recovery → RECOVERED
StateAllowed action / transitionRequired effect
IDLEConfigure → READYNo active session. Start remains disabled until identity, source readiness, and writable storage are valid.
READYStart → RECORDING; invalid input → IDLEReserve a new folder; initialize manifest, streams, and active marker; start clock; open Silence. Failed initialization shall not show RECORDING.
RECORDINGPause → PAUSED; Stop → FINALIZINGPersist samples and events. Enable labels. A short BLE dropout shall retain this state with a separate connection warning.
PAUSEDResume → RECORDING; Stop → FINALIZINGFlush samples captured before Pause. Stop accepting new measurement samples for storage. Record the pause. Keep the clock and event log active. Resume shall start a new recording segment.
FINALIZINGSuccess → COMPLETEFreeze stop time; close segments; flush chunks; verify manifest; run analysis; publish report. Disable recording controls for this session.
COMPLETEOpen report; create next sessionRecording is finalized. Analysis and report are valid. Insufficient-data results are valid results when clearly identified.
INCOMPLETERecover → RECOVEREDPreserve valid chunks. Identify unfinished writes, missing coverage, and the last durable time. Never imply a normal stop.
RECOVEREDAnalyse; regenerate; open report; create next sessionRetain recovery provenance. Do not resume acquisition into the recovered session. Show incomplete coverage.
  • STA-01The application shall derive control availability from session state. It shall reject invalid transitions and repeated Start or Stop requests.
  • STA-02The application shall store recording status separately from analysis and report status. A report failure shall not mark valid raw capture as corrupt.
  • STA-03The application shall expose finalization failures and a retry action. It shall not display COMPLETE before the report is valid.

Proposed failure mapping: after raw commit, keep a report failure as a retryable FINALIZING substatus and release acquisition resources for the next session. A storage failure shall end safe capture as INCOMPLETE. Confirm this mapping in Phase 0; do not create an invisible “stuck” session.

10

Reliability constraints

  • REL-01The recorder shall use bounded memory and persistent chunks. It shall retain the existing 10-second target chunk duration. It shall measure durable-write lag.
  • REL-02The writer shall write to a temporary file, verify the write, and publish by atomic rename. It shall use a documented durability policy. A failed write shall not replace a valid chunk.
  • REL-03The manifest shall index committed chunks and checksums. Opening a session shall detect missing, duplicate, or corrupt chunks. Valid chunks shall remain accessible.
  • REL-04The application shall maintain an active-session marker. Startup recovery shall reconcile valid chunks with the manifest. It shall identify partial chunks and truncated event tails without overwriting them.
  • REL-05The application shall record disconnect and reconnect boundaries. It shall attempt reconnection. It shall preserve the elapsed timeline without fabricated samples.
  • REL-06Pause shall stop storage of new physiological and audio samples. Resume shall not backfill paused samples. Analysis shall exclude pause gaps.
  • REL-07A disk, permission, queue, or stream error shall be visible and recorded where storage permits. The application shall never continue to claim successful recording after silent data loss.
  • REL-08A report or analysis failure shall leave committed raw data intact. Retrying derived work shall not require another measurement session.
  • REL-09A 60-minute session shall not require its complete data in RAM. Quality flags shall not delete poor-quality raw data.
Durability gate. The original plan requests a normal unsaved interval below 10 seconds and a target chunk duration of 10 seconds. Phase 0 shall select a flush schedule with enough margin for writing and syncing. The agent shall measure the bound; a chunk timer alone does not prove it.
