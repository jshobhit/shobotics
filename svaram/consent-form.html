01

About the study

Svaram and Shobotics study physical and mental responses to Sonorium sound sessions. This is not diagnosis or treatment.

Visit: 90 minutes, including setup and the post-session survey.

02

Your visit

  1. Forms. Complete the questionnaire and consent form. You may skip survey questions.
  2. Chest strap. Wear a sanitised Polar H10 under your clothes to record ECG (heart signal), heart rate, time between beats and movement. It uses no needles and sends no electricity into your body. We show you how to fit it and help only with permission. Report tightness or skin irritation.
  3. Room audio. A microphone may capture your voice. Only the study team can listen; identifying audio will not be published or shared.
03

Data and privacy

Coded records. Your participant ID links study records. Linking across studies requires your agreement. Researchers use coded data; reports do not identify you.

Secure storage. Svaram and Shobotics hold study data on a dedicated, encrypted computer. Identity links and signed consent forms are stored separately from research data.
